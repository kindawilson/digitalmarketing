Writing a Quality Search Ad

1. Call-to-Action (CTA)

Your CTA should be clear, direct, and action-oriented. A strong CTA helps convert interest into clicks.

ShopOrder NowLearn MoreGet StartedRequest a QuoteDownloadTry for FreeStart TodaySee PricingAdd to Cart
Tip: Tailor the CTA to the buyer-journey stage. Are they browsing, comparing, or ready to purchase?

2. Highlight Product Benefits

Benefits make the offer appealing. Tell potential customers why they should click and what sets the product apart.

Free ShippingSustainable MaterialsFree Returns15-inch Laptop SleeveDurable FabricPerfect for Travel24/7 Support
Best practice: Highlight a meaningful differentiator while staying concise.

3. Relevance to the Landing Page

A quality ad does not stop at the click. It prepares the visitor for the landing-page experience.

  • Use language that matches the page's keywords, CTA, and benefits.
  • Reinforce the same product benefit or offer.
  • Make the transition from ad to page seamless and trustworthy.

4. Keyword Relevance

Search ads depend on keywords to match user intent.

  • Use relevant keywords in headlines and descriptions.
  • Choose keywords that match the persona and product.
  • Avoid vague terms; specific language is better.
Example: Replace “bag” with “lightweight hiking backpack with laptop sleeve.”

5. Grammar and Spelling

Typos and poor grammar damage credibility instantly. Every ad should be professionally written, free from errors, clear, and concise.

Review tip: Read the ad aloud to catch awkward phrasing and missed punctuation.

Bidding Strategy: Make Smart Investment Choices

Adjust your bid based on keyword competitiveness.

CompetitionRecommended Strategy
Non-CompetitiveBid at or close to the suggested amount; focus on efficiency and relevance.
LowBid at or close to the suggested amount and strengthen the ad copy.
MediumBid 3-5% higher.
HighBid 10-15% higher.

Final Pre-Launch Checklist

  • Have I included a clear CTA?
  • Did I mention at least one strong product benefit?
  • Is the language consistent with the landing page?
  • Are relevant keywords used in key placements?
  • Is the grammar and spelling correct?
  • Did I apply the right bidding strategy?
