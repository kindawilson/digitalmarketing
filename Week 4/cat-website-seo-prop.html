How to Keep Your Indoor Cat Happy and Healthy

A comfortable home, daily play, and a predictable routine can help your cat stay active, curious, and content.

By Maya Collins  |  Reviewed by Dr. Lena Ortiz, DVM  |  Updated November 2026

Indoor cats can live rich, healthy lives when their environment supports their natural instincts. A few thoughtful choices can give a cat more opportunities to climb, explore, rest, and connect with people.

Create a Cat-Friendly Home

Give your cat a mix of high perches, quiet hiding spots, scratching surfaces, and comfortable places to sleep. Place these resources in different parts of the home so your cat can choose where to spend time.

Keep Your Cat Active Every Day

Use wand toys, rolling balls, and short games that imitate hunting. Two or three brief play sessions can be more appealing to a cat than one long session. Rotate toys to keep familiar activities interesting.

Build a Calm, Consistent Routine

Cats often feel safest when meals, play, and quiet time happen on a predictable schedule. Watch your cat's body language and allow space when the cat chooses to rest alone.

Small changes can make a big difference. Start with one new activity or resting place, observe what your cat enjoys, and adjust the environment over time.

Quick Cat Tip

Let your cat approach new toys and spaces at its own pace. Choice helps a cat feel secure.
