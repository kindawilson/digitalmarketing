Digital Advertising

Just as different areas of digital marketing can have different purposes, the same is true for digital ads. There are different types of ads that work well for different industries, different customers, or even different points in the customer behavior process.

This module will cover ads, their upsides and downsides, and their uses.

We will also have a special focus on search ads that will relate to this module’s simulation assignment.
