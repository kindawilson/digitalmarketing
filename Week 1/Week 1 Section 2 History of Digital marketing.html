What This Means for Digital Marketers

I’m sure you noticed how much monumental innovation has occurred just within the last couple of decades. What does this mean for us as digital marketers? For one thing, digital marketing is a field where it’s definitely important to stay up to date on what’s happening.

But let’s go beyond that. With the rate of change that’s happening, it’s impossible to learn one thing today and assume it won’t change for the next decade—or even year. The breadth of everything in digital marketing is so wide that it would be almost impossible to memorize it all, and even if you did, something would undoubtedly change soon.

What is constant are the foundational principles underlying a digital marketing strategy. In this class, we will establish a framework of digital marketing core principles. We will build on that by learning how to apply some of the newest, cutting-edge tools and tactics. As you continue your marketing journey in the business world, you can rely on the framework we learn while adjusting for new updates and innovations.
