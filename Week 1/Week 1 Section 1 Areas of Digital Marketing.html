Section 1

Areas of Digital Marketing

Explore how individual digital channels work together to create a connected marketing strategy.

Key Takeaways

  • Digital marketing is a connected ecosystem of brand touchpoints—not a single channel or tactic.
  • Effective strategies begin with strong foundations and coordinate five core areas: Website/UX/UI, SEO, Content Marketing, Social Media, and Digital Ads.

What do you think of when you hear the term digital marketing? Maybe you think of social media—a large part of our everyday digital lives. Or maybe you think of advertising, much like people often equate advertising with the word marketing in general.

In reality, digital marketing has grown to encompass many things, including social media, digital ads, and websites.

Think of all of the places online where you come into contact with a brand—or its touchpoints: You might see an ad for the brand online, search for it on Google, then go to its website, start following it on social media, sign up for its email list, and watch its videos on YouTube. When we talk about digital marketing, we need to look at all of those pieces in the digital world and develop our strategy.

DefinitionSo we can use this definition of digital marketing: digital marketing is “the use of websites, apps, mobile devices, social media, search engines, and other digital means to promote and sell products and services.”

The first thing we will go over in class is some of the foundational items that any marketer needs to establish before launching into a digital marketing strategy. Without these items, you won’t be able to fully or effectively create the pieces of your digital strategy.

After we have established those foundational items, we’ll focus on five main areas of digital marketing: Website/UX/UI, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Content Marketing, Social Media, and Digital Ads. We’ll step through each of them and use each piece to develop an overall digital marketing plan for a company.
