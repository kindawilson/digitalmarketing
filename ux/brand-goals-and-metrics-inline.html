Brand Goals and Metrics

Social media goals guide the content

You can have several types of goals on social media, and other goals, if you will, to get you there. For instance, your goal could be to increase sales. And other related goals could be to increase credibility / image so that people would see you as an expert and want to purchase from you. Another company might be an entertainment company, so post viewing and engagement is the final goal for the company. This will all drive the type of content that you produce.

How do you track success of those goal(s)? You choose the type of metrics that align with them. Here are key metrics that we can track for different social media goals.

What social media analytics metrics should you track?

The metrics you track should align with your business goals. Tracking everything leads to dashboard overload. Tracking the right things leads to better decisions. Here’s a framework that maps common business goals to the metrics that actually matter.

Source for this section Content below from and adapted from: https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-analytics
Business goal Key metrics Why it matters
Brand awarenessReach, impressions, follower growth, share of voiceShows how many people are seeing your brand and whether that audience is growing
EngagementEngagement rate, comments, shares, savesReveals whether your content resonates enough for people to interact with it
ConversionsClick-through rate, conversion rate, cost per conversion, revenue attributedConnects social activity directly to business outcomes like leads and sales
Brand healthBrand mentions, sentiment trend, share of voice vs. competitorsTracks your reputation over time and flags potential issues early
Customer experienceResponse time, sentiment score, CSAT (customer satisfaction score)Measures how well your social team supports and satisfies customers
AudienceWho follows you and how fast they growFollower count, follower growth rate, demographics

Choose a focused set of KPIs

Source for this section Content below from and adapted from: https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-analytics

A good rule of thumb: choose 3-5 KPIs per goal. That’s enough to get a clear picture without drowning in data. Your engagement rate and conversion metrics will likely be the ones leadership cares about most, so make sure those are front and center in your reporting.

The metrics you choose will also depend on the platform. A high save rate matters on Instagram. Watch time matters on YouTube. Shares matter on LinkedIn. Tailor your tracking to each channel’s strengths.

Glossary of important social media metrics to track

While the right metrics depend on your goals, these 10 consistently appear in the measurement frameworks used by marketing teams. Each one answers a specific question about your social media performance.
