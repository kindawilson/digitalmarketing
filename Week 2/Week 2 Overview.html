Week 2 Overview

Where do we even start when we want to create a digital marketing strategy? It is tempting to dive right in and start running ads and designing websites. However, it is important to compile some necessary foundational work first—and possibly do some background work and research to get you there.

This module will outline the items that need to be fully researched and clarified to develop the best digital marketing strategy possible.
