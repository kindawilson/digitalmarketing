Section 1

The Foundation

Start with the brand, customer, and goals before choosing digital marketing channels and tactics.

Key Takeaways

  • Strong digital strategies begin with a clear understanding of the brand, the customer, and the goal.
  • These foundational decisions shape every channel that follows, from the website and SEO to content, social media, advertising, and email.

Here are the different foundational elements that will be crucial when building your strategy. Click through brand, customer, and goals below to see what they include.

As you will see, the information that you gather for this foundation will affect every element of your digital marketing framework, including the website, SEO, social media, digital ads, content marketing, and email marketing.

You may be working at a company that already has a firm grasp on its brand identity, mission, customer profile and behavior, and campaign goals. In that case, this section provides an overview of the items you need to have ready to create an effective digital marketing strategy.

If your company does not know some of these items yet, delve into research and gather as much detail as possible. The more in-depth and specific the information, the better.

I cannot tell you how many times I have sat before a company, organization, or even a student team and asked questions such as, “What specific message do you want to get across? What is your brand image? Who exactly is your customer?” Some answered easily, and some did not have any idea. I knew the latter group had quite a bit more work to do.

Let’s look at each of these foundational elements to determine what we need and how to get it.
