Section 4

Digital Marketing Campaign Goals

Choose a focused outcome that guides the audience, message, channels, and measures.

Key Takeaways

  • Campaign goals should describe the specific result the organization needs, not simply name a tactic.
  • Companies may share an ultimate business goal while needing very different digital marketing strategies to achieve it.

At some point in developing your strategy, you should establish your specific goals. If you are running digital marketing long term, you might consider short-term goals for the upcoming quarter or season. You might also have a specific goal for an upcoming campaign.

Possible Digital Marketing Goals

  • Increase brand awareness
  • Create top-of-mind awareness
  • Boost sales
  • Increase website traffic or online views
  • Build or clarify the brand position
  • Establish credibility or quality
  • Rebrand or reposition
  • Build relationships
  • Generate leads
  • Provide value

For most companies, increasing sales in some form will be the ultimate goal. That is why they are in business. However, the strategy to reach that goal will look very different across product life cycle stages and types of products.

For instance, both Pepsi and a brand-new drink called SOTHIRSTY may want to increase sales. Only one of those brands will need to increase basic customer awareness in the United States market. That distinction matters because strategies for building awareness differ from strategies for gaining credibility in the market.

For an entertainment company, the goal may be to maximize views rather than sell a product directly.

For a business-to-business (B2B) company, goals might include generating leads and building relationships.

Choose the goal before the tactics. A clear goal helps you select the right audience, message, channel, content, and measurement plan.
