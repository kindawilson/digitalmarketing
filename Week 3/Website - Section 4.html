Putting it all together: A page template

Effective website landing pages often follow a simple pattern because that structure works with human attention and decision-making.

Look at these examples from websites - what do you see in common? It almost appears like they're following a template, and it's because this type of layout is effective, given all of the psychology we've covered so far.

For an effective website home/landing page

Answer the questions:

  1. Who / what are you?
  2. Why should customers buy from you?
  3. What to do next

For the design "above the fold"

  • Remember KISS - Keep It Simple, Stupid
  • Include a vivid, compelling image
  • Put the most important thing in a short headline
  • Chunk a few bits of supporting information
  • Provide a clear call to action
  • (and leave plenty of whitespace)

...and that's it!
