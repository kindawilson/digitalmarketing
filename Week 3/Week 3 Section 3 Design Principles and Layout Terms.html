Section 3

Design Principles and Layout Terms

Use visual relationships to make information easier to scan, understand, and remember.

Key Takeaways

  • Alignment and repetition create order and consistency.
  • Hierarchy, contrast, and proximity communicate importance and relationships.
  • White space and sufficient color contrast improve focus and readability.

Open this lesson full screen

These principles apply to websites, landing pages, ads, email, and other digital materials. Notice how they overlap with the Laws of UX—especially hierarchy, contrast, and proximity.

Six Basic Principles

  1. Alignment: connect elements along consistent edges or axes.
  2. Hierarchy: show which information is most important.
  3. Contrast: make meaningfully different elements stand apart.
  4. Proximity: group related elements close together.
  5. Space: give content room to breathe.
  6. Repetition: reuse templates, colors, and patterns consistently.

Alignment

Alignment creates a sharp, ordered appearance by giving elements a clear visual connection. If a design feels subtly wrong, check whether text and objects share intentional edges.

Hierarchy

Visual hierarchy gives more weight to the most important message. You can create it with scale, weight, contrast, position, spacing, or shapes.

Try this: squint at your design. Is the most important message still the first thing you can identify? Then ask someone unfamiliar with the content what they read first and second.

Space

The parts of a design left blank are as important as the filled areas. White space, also called negative space, creates shape, separates groups, and helps important information stand out.

Color Tips

  • Use color to emphasize actions and support navigation.
  • Use a consistent brand palette and understand the associations colors carry.
  • Maintain sufficient contrast between text and its background; smaller type generally needs stronger contrast.
  • Avoid jarring combinations and test contrast for people with low vision or color-vision deficiencies.
